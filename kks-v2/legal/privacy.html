Who this applies to

This notice explains how KKS handles personal information of website visitors, job applicants and the business contacts of our agency clients.

Information we collect

Contact details you submit through forms, basic analytics about website use, and information our agency clients provide to us so we can perform services on their behalf.

How we use it

To respond to enquiries, deliver services under our client agreements, assess job applications and keep our services secure.

Client data

When we work for an agency, we act on its instructions. Data stays in the agency’s systems wherever possible and is only accessed by assigned team members.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us operate, or where required by law.

Retention

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law and client contracts.
