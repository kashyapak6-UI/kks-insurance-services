What We Handle

The Work, Owned End to End

Each task follows a written procedure and passes a QA check before it reaches your producer, client or carrier.

01

Auto liability & cargo quoting

New venture and experienced fleet submissions, including driver lists, IFTA and loss history.

02

Vehicle & driver schedules

Units and drivers added or deleted quickly, with schedules reconciled against the carrier every month.

03

Filings support

Federal and state filing requests tracked with the carrier so authority stays active.

04

Certificates for shippers & brokers

Same-day certificates for freight brokers and shippers with the right cargo and auto limits.

05

Physical damage & reefer coverage

Stated values, lienholders and reefer breakdown endorsements kept up to date.

06

Audits & mileage reports

Mileage and revenue reports gathered for audits, with discrepancies explained before they become bills.

Workflow

How a Request Moves Through Our Desk

  1. 01
    Unit or driver changeThe fleet manager emails a change, a photo of the title or a new driver list.
  2. 02
    Verify & processWe check VINs, values and driver details, then submit the endorsement to the carrier.
  3. 03
    Certificates refreshedAny certificate holders affected by the change get an updated COI automatically.
  4. 04
    Monthly reconciliationSchedules are compared with carrier records every month so audit time holds no surprises.
