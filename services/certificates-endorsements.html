What We Handle

The Work, Owned End to End

Each task follows a written procedure and passes a QA check before it reaches your producer, client or carrier.

01

Certificates of insurance

Standard and project-specific COIs issued from your certificate system with the right holder and description.

02

Additional insured & waivers

Requests checked against policy forms; endorsements requested from the carrier when needed.

03

Contract requirement reviews

Insurance requirements in contracts compared with the policy, and gaps flagged to the producer.

04

Holder list management

Master holder lists kept current and renewal certificates sent automatically.

05

Policy endorsements

Coverage, limit, location and named insured changes requested and confirmed.

06

Compliance tracking

Expiring certificates for vendors and subcontractors tracked for clients that need it.

Workflow

How a Request Moves Through Our Desk

  1. 01
    Request receivedClients or producers send requests by email or portal; we log each one with a timestamp.
  2. 02
    Check the policyWe confirm limits, forms and endorsements actually support what is being certified.
  3. 03
    Issue & QAThe certificate is issued and a second specialist checks it before sending.
  4. 04
    Send & fileThe COI goes to the holder and requester and is filed against the client record.
